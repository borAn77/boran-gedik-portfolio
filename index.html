WhyTired

A health and training assistant for students who train often and feel tired without knowing why. A daily check-in gives a hard, easy or rest recommendation.

  • Suggests a 7-day experiment to test the most likely cause
  • Prepares a one-page summary in Polish for the family doctor
  • All data in the demo is synthetic
PythonFastAPIReactTypeScriptTailwind

Backend · Satellite data · Water quality

WaterShield

A system that monitors EU river water quality using Sentinel-2 satellite imagery. A data pipeline processes the imagery, AI anomaly detection triggers risk alerts, and a compliance module produces EU Water Framework Directive reports. Time-series data lives in a Supabase / PostgreSQL schema, and REST APIs serve the frontend.

PythonFastAPISupabasePostgreSQLSentinel-2

What I did: I was the sole backend developer and built the whole server side with FastAPI in 48 hours: the pipeline, anomaly detection, database schema, REST APIs and the compliance report module.
