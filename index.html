WaterShield

Real-time water pollution detection and forecasting across 102 European rivers in 32 countries. It detects pollution from Sentinel-2 satellite imagery (Copernicus), forecasts the Water Quality Index 7–30 days ahead with XGBoost (RMSE 11.9), and flags anomalies in live sensor data with IsolationForest.
